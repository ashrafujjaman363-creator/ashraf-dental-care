Frequently asked questions

Does a root canal hurt?

The tooth is gently numbed before treatment, so you feel no pain during the procedure.

How much does treatment cost?

The cost depends on the treatment. At your first check-up the specialist will give you a clear estimate.

How often should I get my teeth cleaned?

A scaling and routine check-up every six months is recommended for healthy gums.

What should I do if I have sudden tooth pain?

Rinse with lukewarm water, avoid pressing on the sore spot, and message us on WhatsApp straight away.

Can I pay with bKash or Nagad?

Yes. We accept cash, bKash, Nagad and all major cards.
